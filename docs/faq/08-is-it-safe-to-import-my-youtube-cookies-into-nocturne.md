# FAQ: Is it safe to import my YouTube cookies into Nocturne?

## Question
Is it safe to import my YouTube cookies into Nocturne?

## Answer
Yes. Cookies are stored locally on your device in hardware-backed Android Keystore encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences). They are used strictly to sign YouTube InnerTube requests for your library and playlists, and are never sent to third-party analytics or external servers.
