# FAQ: How do I transfer my Nocturne settings and playlists to a new phone?

## Question
How do I transfer my Nocturne settings and playlists to a new phone?

## Answer
Open Nocturne Settings -> Backup & Restore -> Export Backup. The app creates a compressed .nocturne backup archive containing your playlists, favorites, custom EQ presets, and history. Transfer this file to your new device and select Restore Backup to seamlessly recover everything.
