# FAQ: Why does the equalizer support parametric biquad bands?

## Question
Why does the equalizer support parametric biquad bands?

## Answer
Graphic equalizers with fixed center frequencies cannot correct specific acoustic resonances in headphones. Parametric biquad filters allow users to freely adjust center frequency, gain (dB), and Q-factor (bandwidth), enabling precise audiophile Harman curve tuning and headphone correction.
